Install Swisstopo maps on your Garmin watch

Works on Epix 2 and Fenix 7 Pro. Read the section at the bottom if you have a Fenix 6. No special software needed — we just copy files.

Step 1 — Pick which maps you want

  • GPX picker — drop a GPX of your tour and get direct download links for the right zone.
  • Tile picker — paint your own area and have a custom pack built (5–15 min).

Always also download 00_overview_ch.kmz (the all-CH background). The GPX picker links to it automatically.

Step 2 — Plug the watch into your computer

Use the USB cable that came with the watch. It appears as a USB drive named GARMIN. If nothing shows up, try a different USB port — many phone-charging cables don't carry data.

Step 3 — Open the Garmin/CustomMaps/ folder

On the GARMIN drive, navigate to:

GARMIN/Garmin/CustomMaps/

(Yes, the word Garmin appears twice.) If CustomMaps/ doesn't exist, create it. The name must be exactly CustomMaps (capital C, capital M, no space).

Step 4 — Clean out old .kmz files

✅ DELETE every file inside CustomMaps/ that ends in .kmz. Those are old maps. They steal your tile budget.
❌ DO NOT DELETE any .img file, anywhere on the GARMIN drive. Those are your built-in maps (TopoActive, basemap). Deleting them breaks the watch and you'll need a factory reset.

Folders to leave completely alone, even if they look empty:

  • Garmin/Maps/
  • Garmin/Maps/MapInstall/
  • anything containing .img, .gma, .unl, or .jnx

Rule of thumb: only delete files ending in .kmz, and only inside Garmin/CustomMaps/.

Step 5 — Copy the new .kmz files in

Drag every .kmz you downloaded into Garmin/CustomMaps/. Overview + regional all go in the same folder.

GARMIN/
└── Garmin/
    └── CustomMaps/
        ├── 00_overview_ch.kmz
        ├── 02_wallis_west_part_001.kmz
        ├── 02_wallis_west_part_002.kmz
        ├── 02_wallis_west_part_003.kmz
        └── 02_wallis_west_part_004.kmz
Don't mix zones. The watch caps at ~500 tiles across all KMZs combined. One overview + one regional zone uses almost the whole budget. Extra zones get silently dropped.

Step 6 — Safely eject

  • Windows: right-click GARMIN in This PC → Eject.
  • Mac: drag the drive to the Trash.
  • Linux: right-click → Eject.

Step 7 — Reboot the watch and enable Custom Maps

  1. Hold the Light button (top-left) for ~15 seconds until the watch powers off, then press it again to power back on.
  2. Hold the back button → Map → Map Settings → Map Layers (sometimes labelled "Configure Map" or "Configure Maps").
  3. Make sure Custom Maps is On.

Done. Open the map screen — the LK25 raster with slope shading and ski-tour routes is there.

⚠ Individual KMZs do not appear in Map Manager. Map Manager only lists built-in .img map families. KMZs render as one combined layer with one toggle. Swap regions by moving files via USB.

Switching regions later

  1. Plug in, open Garmin/CustomMaps/.
  2. Delete the old regional .kmz (keep 00_overview_ch.kmz).
  3. Copy the new region's .kmz in.
  4. Eject, reboot.

Troubleshooting

Map looks blank. Did you reboot? Did you enable Map Layers → Custom Maps? Are the .kmz in Garmin/CustomMaps/ and not Garmin/Maps/?

Only part of the region renders. Over the 500-tile cap — keep only one regional pack at a time.

Watch can't show maps at all. An .img was probably deleted. Reinstall the basemap via Garmin Express.

Watch hangs on boot. Unplug, hold Light 30 s, retry. If persistent, remove the most recently added .kmz.

Fenix 6 / 6 Pro / 6X Pro

The current packs don't fit on the Fenix 6 family — those watches cap at 100 tiles total (vs 500 on Epix 2 / Fenix 7 Pro) and 3 MB per KMZ. A coarser Fenix 6 build is planned but not yet shipped. Until then, the GPX picker still works for orientation; for the actual map on the watch you'd fall back to Garmin TOPO Schweiz v2 PRO.
